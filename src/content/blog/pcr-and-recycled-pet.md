EHON can review PCR materials and recycled PET for selected cosmetic packaging programs. The right question is not “do you have sustainable packaging?” It is “can this component, this finish and this formula use it?”

## Where it is realistic

Clear or lightly colored bottles and selected jars are the usual candidates. A heavy metallic mascara cap or a living hinge is a different material problem. EHON will say when a recycled spec fights clarity, color or decoration.

## What to approve on sample

Look at clarity, color drift and how stamp, print or spray sits on the part. A recycled claim that only exists on a slide is not a specification. The sample is.

If recycled content is optional, ask for two routes in one quotation: the standard resin and the recycled alternative. That keeps the launch moving if one route cannot hold the finish.
