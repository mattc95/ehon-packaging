EHON meets beauty brands and sourcing teams at China Beauty Expo in Shanghai. The point of the booth is the same as the factory: packaging you can hold, not only a catalog PDF.

## What to review on site

Bring the categories you are actually launching. Lipstick cases, lip gloss tubes, mascara and eyeliner, eyeshadow and powder compacts, loose powder, cream jars, lotion bottles and accessories can be discussed against your quantity and finish.

## Book the meeting before you fly

Use the quote form or WhatsApp and write “China Beauty Expo” in the project note, plus the products you want on the table. If you cannot attend, the same brief goes to the Shantou team and can continue by WeChat, Line or email.

Exhibition pages stay useful after the show. Project conversations started in Shanghai continue as sampling and production at the factory.
