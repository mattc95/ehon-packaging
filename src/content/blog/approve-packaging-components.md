Approve the component you will reorder. A plain shot and a decorated render are not that component.

## Fit

Check threads, cups, wipers, hinges, sifters and pumps on the real parts. If an accessory comes from the same factory, approve it on the primary pack.

## Appearance

Color, gloss and logo position move after spray, metallizing or foil. Sign the decorated sample. Note the finish by name: hot stamping, silk screen, UV metallizing, matte, UV coating, gradient, transfer or laser.

## Documents

EHON’s published systems are ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018. Ask for the copy your retailer portal needs, and keep the approved sample as the quality reference for bulk.
